## Frame

Problem: Text documents are read in 250-line windows behind Previous lines / Line / Next lines buttons, and PDFs one page at a time behind a toolbar of page buttons, zoom and search (OBSERVED: viewer-context.tsx TextViewer, pdf-viewer.tsx; the user's screenshot of a tab with "Previous lines", "Line 1", "Lines 1-250 of 314", "Next lines"). The user wants the document window as simple as possible and another way to navigate (STATED: "sa gasim o modalitate pentru navigarea in document (previous lines, next lines...) ... ferastra de afisat documentul sa fie cat mai simpla").
Desired outcome: Text and logs scroll continuously over the whole file with line numbers, rendering only the lines near the view, with Ctrl+G go to line and Ctrl+F find shown only when asked; PDFs scroll continuously through their pages, rendering only pages near the view, with a small page indicator, Ctrl+wheel and Ctrl+= / Ctrl+- zoom and Ctrl+F find shown only when asked (STATED: answers "Scroll continuu + Ctrl+G" and "Scroll continuu prin pagini"; parent RULING-005). Cited lines, pages and snippets stay highlighted as today.
Actors: the embedded engineer reading logs and datasheets in document tabs.
Primary scenario: a 600 000-line UART log opens at its cited line 412 380 with that line highlighted; scrolling moves through the whole file without buttons; Ctrl+G 1000 Enter shows line 1000; Ctrl+F "ERR" highlights matches and Enter moves to the next. A 300-page datasheet opens at cited page 47 with the snippet highlighted and "47 / 300" shown; scrolling continues to page 48; Ctrl+= zooms in; Ctrl+F "VDD" moves through matches across pages.
Examples: a 314-line Markdown-as-text file scrolls as one list with no toolbar. A PDF whose cited range is 12-14 opens at page 12.
Counterexamples: Markdown, HTML, office and artifact views are unchanged (they already scroll continuously; OBSERVED: DocumentBody sections).
In scope: continuous text viewer with virtual rendering, Ctrl+G, Ctrl+F find for text; continuous PDF viewer with lazy page rendering, page indicator, zoom keys and wheel, Ctrl+F find across pages, cited snippet highlight; the places kept per tab (TASK-79f6ffd3) adapted to the new viewers; en/ro/de.
Out of scope: editing; live reload or tail of growing logs; persistence (child 3, TASK-061fe00e); the tab menu (child 2).
Constraints: as the parent; the DOM stays bounded for large files (OBSERVED: the existing "bounds large text DOM" test); the pdf.js worker and assets stay pinned and local (knowledge document-viewer.md); no new dependency unless the design shows one is needed.
Success signals: no Previous/Next toolbars; scrolling reaches every line and page; Ctrl+G, Ctrl+F and zoom work from the keyboard; cited places open highlighted; large files stay responsive.
Evidence: viewer-context.tsx (TextViewer, DocumentBody), pdf-viewer.tsx, REQ-UX-28ad0002, documents.test.ts "bounds large text DOM" read 2026-11-01.
Assumptions: none beyond the labelled claims.
Unknowns: none blocking; the line height and wrapping strategy for virtual rendering are design choices.
Decision-blocking questions: none (RULING-005).
Proposed behaviors: (accepted in specify under autopilot) Escape closes an open find or go-to field before it returns to Chat; Ctrl+0 resets PDF zoom; the page indicator shows the page whose area fills most of the view.

## Design

**Approach.** Text: a new `TextViewer` (documents/text-viewer.tsx) lays every line out at one
measured height without wrapping, so the scroll height is `lines x height` and only the lines in view
plus 40 on each side are rendered (REQ-UX-8a994f4b, REQ-NFR-0fe89e49); it opens at the kept first
visible line, else with the cited range a third down the view. PDF: `PdfViewer` reads every page's
size once after loading, stacks pages in one scroll and renders canvas plus text layer only for the
page in view and its neighbours (at most five), with a `page / pages` indicator (REQ-UX-e4d5ce0f);
cited snippets and find matches are marked with the existing `highlightText`. Zoom steps 50-200%
with Ctrl+wheel (a non-passive listener), Ctrl+= / Ctrl+- and Ctrl+0, keeping the page in view
(REQ-UX-e8dfdec3). A shared `FindBar` (documents/find-bar.tsx) appears on Ctrl+F (text and PDF,
REQ-UX-fe9ec5c2, REQ-UX-3eb27a6c) or Ctrl+G (text, REQ-UX-58cf59b2): Enter and Shift+Enter step,
Escape closes it and stays in the tab. `DocumentPanel` turns Ctrl+F and Ctrl+G into request counters
passed down through `DocumentBody`. Rejected: wrapping lines with measured heights (heavier
virtualization) and IntersectionObserver-driven rendering (layout math is enough and testable).

**Interfaces and data.** Places: text `{line}` (first visible line), PDF `{page}` (page in view),
other kinds `{scroll}` (REQ-UX-1f53a583). `DocumentBody({content, place, onPlace, findRequest,
gotoRequest})`. No toolbars: the PDF page buttons, page field, zoom select and the text window
buttons go (REQ-UX-28ad0002 revised).

**Files and test seams.** text-viewer.tsx and find-bar.tsx (new), pdf-viewer.tsx (rewritten),
viewer-context.tsx (DocumentBody wiring, old TextViewer removed), document-tabs.tsx (Ctrl+F/G
requests), styles.css, locales. Tests (run at the end, parent RULING-006): documents.test.ts,
document-tabs.test.ts and new viewer tests; e2e citations, documents, pdf-download.

**Risks and open decisions.** Long log lines scroll horizontally instead of wrapping. Page sizes are
read for every page at load (one getPage per page).

**Requirement map.** REQ-UX-8a994f4b, REQ-UX-58cf59b2, REQ-UX-fe9ec5c2, REQ-NFR-0fe89e49 text-viewer;
REQ-UX-e4d5ce0f, REQ-UX-e8dfdec3, REQ-UX-3eb27a6c pdf-viewer; REQ-UX-28ad0002 and REQ-UX-1f53a583
revised wording, places as above.
